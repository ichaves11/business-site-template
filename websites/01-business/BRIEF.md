# Business template redesign

Authored implementation decisions under the user's request to recreate a more professional template using the supplied references. Existing brief supplies the service-business scope. Latest user instruction: "we are creating a template website so no specific things".

## Brief
1. Vibe: precise, spacious, dimensional, botanical green. User references: Foresee and Audience Map.
2. Journey: understand the offer, recognize disconnected growth, choose a goal, explore the delivery, download a useful brief.
3. Energy: confident opening, quiet explanation, expansive interactive peak, practical delivery, calm questions, resolved action.
4. Feelings: curiosity → recognition → agency → confidence → clarity → resolve.
5. Signature: Growth Route. Selecting a goal reroutes a visual diagram, rewrites the recommended actions, and carries those choices into a downloadable brief.
6. Aesthetic: premium product marketing, green/ivory, large clean sans type, restrained soft edges.
7. Distinct scenes with natural scroll. No prolonged pinned narrative or horizontal scrolling.
8. Assets: existing Scroll Craft engine, original SVG growth system, one licensed Unsplash forest photograph if available, locally hosted font. No generated assets, no paid APIs. No client logos, fabricated statistics or testimonials.

## Reference observations
Foresee: compact floating navigation, centred readable opening, layered green atmosphere, interactive demonstration below the claim, hard cuts into pale explanatory sections.
Audience Map: photographic green opening, lime action accent, substantial white space, dark immersive product moments. Adapt material depth and hierarchy, not its copy or product identity.

## Grammar
New grammar: Guided service configurator. Navigation jumps to offer, route and questions; hero introduces the business promise; the central interactive route is the largest section; the ending exports the visitor's selection. Bans: forced pinning, passive mock dashboards, fake proof, full-page app chrome, generic feature card grid.
Other grammars: filmic imposes a long linear argument; editorial prioritizes prose; live surface forbids useful marketing explanation; continuous world has no geographic justification; typographic poster omits the useful route; catalog requires equal object labels; split stage imposes a comparison; rhythmic cutlist is too fast for decision-making.

## Feeling curve (before device score)
Opening: curiosity, a forest ground and separated orbit planes frame one concise promise.
Introduction: recognition, a plain-language statement identifies disconnected tools.
Growth Route: agency, a chosen goal transforms a connected route and concrete next steps. Peak: "I chose what was holding us back and the page mapped out what to build."
Services: confidence, expandable service detail connected to a bespoke illustration.
FAQ: clarity, concise honest process answers.
Close: resolve, the selected route becomes a downloadable brief.
Tell-someone: It's the site where you turn a growth problem into a project plan you can take away.
No authored dead scroll or empty holds.

## Layer contract
Far forest: slow natural-flow translation, decorative, dark image with clean copy region.
Mid orbit arcs: independent SVG scale/rotation, lower hero only.
Focal Studio monogram: stable centred ring, separate foreground paths establish overlap.
Near connection labels: constrained pointer displacement, no headline overlap.
Typography: stable real HTML, complete headline and action always visible.
Mobile: title above orbit, smaller diagram and labels, normal flow, no pointer dependence.
Reduced motion: fixed layer composition; all content remains readable.
